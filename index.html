Odyssey Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2, 2026

Thank you for playing Odyssey. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled while you use the game.

Information We Collect

Odyssey is designed to collect as little information as possible. Depending on the features enabled, the game may collect:

  • Anonymous crash reports.
  • Device information (device model and operating system).
  • Anonymous gameplay analytics.
  • Advertising identifiers (only if advertisements are enabled).

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or photos through the game.

Game Data

Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device unless cloud save is explicitly introduced in a future version.

Advertisements

If advertisements are enabled, third-party providers such as Google AdMob may collect anonymous information to serve ads and measure advertising performance.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

Odyssey may use services such as Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to improve game stability, identify bugs, and enhance gameplay.

Permissions

Depending on the version of the game, Odyssey may request:

  • Internet access.
  • Network state access.

The game does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, or location.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Games (optional)
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google AdMob (if enabled)

Children's Privacy

Odyssey is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information handled by the game. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
